Our services

Comprehensive healthcare solutions tailored to your needs, delivered online with medication sent to your door.

✅ Often suitable for an online consultation

  • • Colds, flu and sinus symptoms
  • • Sore throat and mild fever
  • • Allergies and hay fever
  • • Skin rashes (with photos)
  • • Urinary tract infection symptoms
  • • Chronic condition check-ins
  • • Repeat prescription reviews
  • • Test result discussions

Your doctor will tell you if you need to be seen in person.

🚑 Not for online care: get emergency help

  • • Chest pain, difficulty breathing or signs of stroke
  • • Severe bleeding, burns, head injuries or broken bones
  • • Loss of consciousness, seizures or severe confusion
  • • Suicidal thoughts. Call the SADAG helpline on 0800 567 567

Call 10177 (ambulance) or 112 from a cellphone.
