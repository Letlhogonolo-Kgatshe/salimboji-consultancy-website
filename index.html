🔒 Your privacy

Your health information is confidential and handled in line with POPIA.

When online care isn't right

Go to your nearest emergency unit or call 10177 (or 112 from a cellphone) for:

  • Chest pain or difficulty breathing
  • Signs of a stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech)
  • Severe bleeding, burns or injuries
  • Loss of consciousness or seizures
